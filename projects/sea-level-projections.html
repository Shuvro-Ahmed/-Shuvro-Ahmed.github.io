Physics-informed transfer learning for relative sea-level projections

Physics-informed modelling and cross-regional transfer learning for relative sea-level projections in Bangladesh, Vietnam, and the Maldives, with vertical land motion and coastal-vulnerability analysis.

01 / Problem

The task

Study regional relative sea-level projections for Bangladesh, Vietnam, and the Maldives, including differences in data availability and vertical land motion.

02 / Method

The approach

The manuscript combines an LSTM-based forecasting framework, cross-regional transfer learning, physics-informed training constraints, vertical-land-motion corrections, and SHAP-based analysis. It examines both historical prediction and longer-horizon projection behaviour.

03 / Outcome

What the project produced

A manuscript on relative sea-level forecasting, transfer learning, and coastal vulnerability across Bangladesh, Vietnam, and the Maldives. Submitted and under review.
