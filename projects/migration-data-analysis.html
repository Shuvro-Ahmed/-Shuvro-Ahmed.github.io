Climate-related migration: data analysis and visualization

Python-based analysis and visualization of migration drivers in Bangladesh, including Sankey diagrams.

01 / Problem

The task

Examine the climate, socioeconomic, and other reported factors associated with migration.

02 / Method

The approach

Analyzed migration data in Python and used visual summaries, including Sankey diagrams, to examine reported drivers in the Rajshahi study context.

03 / Outcome

What the project produced

Python analysis and visualizations for the study “Migration in the Lens of Sustainable Adaptation: A Tale of Two Slums in Bangladesh.”
