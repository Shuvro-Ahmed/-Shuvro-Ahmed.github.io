Retail hyperautomation: forecasting, placement, and a voice bot

Hybrid sales forecasting, association-rule mining, and a voice interface for supermarket operations.

Project area

01 / Problem

The task

Explore machine-learning approaches to product demand, product placement, and customer queries in a supermarket.

02 / Method

The approach

Investigated a hybrid linear regression–XGBoost forecasting model, compared association-rule mining algorithms including FP-Growth, and developed a voice bot using NLP and speech tools.

03 / Outcome

What the project produced

The published study reports linear–XGBoost MAE of 68.94, RMSE of 77.48, and MAPE of 0.09. It also reports an FP-Growth runtime of 0.473 seconds for category-level rules at minimum support 0.03.
