Research

Learning from visual worlds.

My interests span visual representation learning, image and video understanding, and 3D vision. I’m interested in building learning systems that make sense of complex visual data—and understanding where those systems succeed or fail.

01 / RESEARCH INTEREST

Representation learning

How visual representations capture structure, and how training objectives shape what they retain.

02 / RESEARCH INTEREST

Image & video understanding

Learning from spatial and temporal evidence to understand images, motion, and unusual observations.

03 / RESEARCH INTEREST

3D vision & perception

Connecting visual reconstruction and novel views with the quality people actually perceive.
